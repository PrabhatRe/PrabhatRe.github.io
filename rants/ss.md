# SS

Silent whispers of night grew morbid  
Darkness drooling from its maws  
The voracious night worm  
Began to devour white sky  

Where proud brilliant radiance shone  
Cast incandescent pale white shadow on the world  
Vanquished the boundary between water and air  
Creating a canvas free and filled with mirth  

As the worm crawled across the canvas  
Blinding darkness filled the world  
Trapped blind in the ooze of dark seawater  
Undulating dark waves began to encroach lungs  

Desperate flames for sky flickered and swayed  

Bewitching spell of darkness  
Covered abyss and sky alike  
With no light to guide  
Darkness found its way  
Filling lungs to its brim  

The veil of darkness let out pale light  
Cold and indifferent as opposed to brilliant light  
Faraway myriad of lanterns gleamed  
The dreamer found his way to sky gasping for light
