# coward

I want to write this for … *i dont know.*  
I wish i could find a **grandiose reason** that i can attribute it to.  
Maybe to lament my nuanced inconveniences, for lack of better words at the moment.

**How long do i write this,**  
what do i write to ache out the livingness in me?  
*or out of me?*

**Hope no one reads this.**

i wish someone reads this and reach out to me 

“ *hey man, this is cool* “

*nah,* i dont want it.

I like to be alone — at times,  
and why?

To look cool?  
To soak in moments of silence and drift aloof in *vague sadness?*

Too exhaustive to assert energy into being,  
wish i knew why.

Why random episodic sadness strap me paralyzed, numb, anxious, nervous.

## Im a coward.

Is he a coward, for he is scared and anxious of trials he might face  
or fears the outcomes he might live on.

I beg not,  
for then world will be full of cowards.

**Im a coward** unable to act for time foregone,  
im a coward among the brave,  
im a coward …

just to attribute *self-hate, self-love,*  
for people to loathe me.

**How long do i have to write?**

It is getting blurry,  
i need to get my eyes checked 

*today? tomorrow?*
