# august

As rain hummed under my umbrella  
Water trickled down my face  
Maybe August reminded me of her face  

As the rain poured,  
Leaving me drenched and cold  
Leaving us cold and drenched  
I still found warmth  
Fizzing around us under my umbrella  

Moments slowly seeped away  

I could see storm brewing southeast  
The howls grew louder  
The winds flew away with my umbrella  

Stood barren on the ground  
Sapped of the warmth  

I could see clouds and beyond  
Stars and light of its own  
As the endless night danced away  

I was all alone on the ground  
Staring as rain brimmed past by me  
Maybe August reminded me of her face
