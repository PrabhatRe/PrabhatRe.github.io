# fisherman

Some clouds stood still some passed by
From the highs and lows of  ferris wheel
Sometimes the sun would glance by
A lonesome fisherman with steel reel
Stood at the coast as his feet swept by

Salt, grey and wide

Where the bait and fishes bickered among
Rowing his hungry daughters far away
Far away into the lands of unborn
Far away into lands of unknown

Quenched, sullen, angry

Fisherman, fished at the sky
Baited his heart to bring the gods out
The drifting clouds sombered in silence
“Are there no gods?”
