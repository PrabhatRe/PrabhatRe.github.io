# how long

how long can you hold your breath for

37 38 39 four..

i can hear my heart beating faster  
screams getting louder and louder  
pumping all it can from getting colder  

faraway i can hear chimes loud and clear  
striking fast, back and forth as i could hear  

they wouldn't listen 

my body wouldn't listen  
the air tasted like blood and spit  
my hands, my legs,  
these hands, these legs,  
left me alone and tired  

we became strangers as we were  

how long can you hold your breath for  
when these hands are at ur neck  
gripping ur soul and flesh together  
waiting for scornful slumber  

how long can you hold me for  
these fragile bones can't hold me longer  

i am scared  
i am scared of heights  
i am scared of this city  
shining under these feet  

though we became strangers as we were  
i wish to smell earth,  
water under this skin  

its getting colder as we speak
